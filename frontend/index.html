How a trade works

Four steps, and the money is protected the whole time.

  1. 1

    Agree on the trade

    Find a verified trader, send a request with the item and price, and wait for them to accept.

  2. 2

    Buyer pays into escrow

    The buyer pays with GCash. The money goes to SecureTrade, not to the seller.

  3. 3

    Seller delivers

    The seller sees the payment is held, hands over the item in-game, and uploads screenshots as proof.

  4. 4

    Seller gets paid

    The buyer checks the item and confirms. If something is wrong, they open a dispute instead.

Built to stop scams

Every part of a trade is designed so neither side can cheat the other.

Verified traders

Everyone verifies with an ID and a selfie before trading, and must be 18 or older.

Money held in escrow

The seller never touches the payment until the buyer has the item.

Delivery proof

Sellers must upload screenshots when they deliver, so there is always evidence.

7-day account protection

A sold game account is protected for 7 days, so a seller cannot take it back after being paid.

Fair disputes

An admin reads both sides, the chat, and the proof, then refunds the buyer or pays the seller.

Honest reviews

Questions

What if the seller never delivers?

Your money stays held. If the seller has not delivered within 48 hours of your payment, you can cancel from the trade page and get a full refund. You can also open a dispute, and the seller can cancel at any time, which refunds you in full.

When does the seller get paid?

When the buyer confirms they received the item. If the buyer does nothing, the payment is released automatically 24 hours after delivery, or 7 days after delivery for game accounts.

What if the seller takes back a game account?

Game accounts are protected for 7 days. If the seller takes the account back in that time, open a dispute and the payment stays held while an admin decides.

How do I pay?

With GCash, through Xendit's secure checkout. On a phone, the GCash app opens. On a computer, GCash's payment page opens.

Is my ID safe?

Your ID pictures are private, and only admins see them. They are deleted as soon as an admin decides, and no ID number is saved.

Can other people see who reviewed them?

No. Reviews show the stars, the comment, and whether the writer was the buyer or the seller, but not who wrote them.
