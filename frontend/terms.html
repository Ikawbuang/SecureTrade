Terms of Service

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Draft. These terms describe how SecureTrade works today. They will be reviewed by a lawyer before SecureTrade handles real money.

1. What SecureTrade is

SecureTrade is an escrow service for trades of in-game items, in-game currency, and game accounts between users in the Philippines. The buyer's payment is held by SecureTrade until the trade is finished, then released to the seller or returned to the buyer.

SecureTrade is not the buyer or the seller in any trade, and is not connected to any game publisher. The traders agree on what is sold and for how much.

2. Who can use SecureTrade

  • You must be 18 years old or older.
  • You may have only one account, and the information you give must be true.
  • You must verify your identity with an ID and a selfie before you can trade. An admin checks them.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password and email account safe.

3. How a trade works

  1. One trader sends a request with the item, the price, and the type of item. If nobody answers within 12 hours, the request is cancelled.
  2. After the other trader accepts, the buyer pays with GCash through Xendit, a payment gateway. If the buyer does not pay within 24 hours, the trade is cancelled.
  3. When the payment is held, the seller delivers the item in-game and uploads 1 to 5 screenshots as proof. If the seller does not deliver within 48 hours, the buyer can cancel and get a full refund.
  4. The buyer confirms the item arrived, and the payment is released to the seller. If the buyer does nothing, the payment is released automatically 24 hours after delivery, or 7 days after delivery for game accounts.

Do not hand over an item before the trade page shows the payment is held. Do not pay or deliver outside SecureTrade: those trades are not protected.

4. Fees

Buyers pay no fee. When a trade is completed, SecureTrade keeps a 3% service fee from the seller's share. For example, on a ₱1,000 trade the seller receives ₱970. There is no fee when a trade is cancelled or refunded. A trade can be from ₱1 to ₱100,000.

5. Cancelling and refunds

  • Before payment, either trader can cancel.
  • After payment, the seller can cancel at any time, and the buyer can cancel if the item was not delivered within 48 hours. Either way, the buyer is refunded in full.
  • After delivery, a trade cannot be cancelled. If something is wrong, the buyer opens a dispute.
  • Refunds go back to the GCash account the buyer paid from.

6. Disputes

Either trader can open a dispute while the payment is held. The payment stays held until an admin decides. The admin looks at the trade details, the chat, the delivery screenshots, and the evidence both traders upload, then refunds the buyer or releases the payment to the seller. Upload all the evidence you have: an admin can only decide on what they can see.

7. What is not allowed

  • Selling something you do not own or cannot deliver, including stolen or hacked accounts and items.
  • Lying in a dispute, editing screenshots, or taking back an item or account after you were paid.
  • Using another person's ID, or making more than one account.
  • Threatening, harassing, or scamming other users, or posting abusive reviews.
  • Trying to break, overload, or get around the security of SecureTrade.

Some games do not allow selling items or accounts. You are responsible for following the rules of the game you trade in.

8. Suspension and bans

An admin can suspend or ban an account that breaks these terms. Trades with a suspended or banned account are paused until an admin decides what happens to the payment.

9. Limits of responsibility

SecureTrade protects the payment, not the game item itself. SecureTrade is not responsible for what a game publisher does to an item or account, such as a ban or a reset. SecureTrade is also not responsible for delays caused by GCash, Xendit, or the internet.

10. Testing period

While the yellow test-mode bar is shown, payments use Xendit's test system. Refunds to buyers and payments to sellers are simulated, so no real money moves.

11. Changes and contact

If these terms change, the date at the top changes too. For questions, use the Support page after signing in.
