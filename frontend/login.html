SecureTrade

Trade items without trusting strangers.

We hold the buyer's payment until the item arrives. Nobody pays for nothing, and nobody delivers for free.

  1. 1 Buyer pays into escrow Money goes to SecureTrade, not the seller.
  2. 2 Payment is held Seller sees it is safe and hands over the item in-game.
  3. 3 Buyer confirms receipt Problem? Upload proof and open a dispute.
  4. 4 Seller is paid Funds release, minus a service fee.
