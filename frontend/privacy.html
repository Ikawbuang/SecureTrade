Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Draft. This policy describes how SecureTrade handles data today. It will be reviewed by a lawyer before SecureTrade handles real money.

This policy explains what SecureTrade collects, why, and who can see it, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your name, email, username, profile picture if you add one, and your password, which is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Identity check: a picture of your ID and a selfie holding it. These are deleted as soon as an admin approves or rejects them. No ID number is saved.
  • Trades: items, prices, trade status, the GCash numbers used to pay and to receive money, delivery screenshots, chat messages and pictures, dispute evidence, and reviews.
  • Security: sign-in times, when you were last active, and a record of actions on each trade.

2. Why we collect it

  • To run trades: hold payments, release them, refund buyers, and settle disputes.
  • To keep traders safe: check that every trader is a real person aged 18 or older, and stop scams.
  • To send you codes, account alerts, and updates about your trades, by email and in the Updates list.
  • To follow the law, for example by keeping payment records.

3. Who can see it

  • Other traders see your username, profile picture, ratings, and reviews. Your trading partner also sees the chat and the trade details. Reviews do not show who wrote them.
  • SecureTrade admins see what they need to verify identities, settle disputes, and handle support.
  • Service providers process data for SecureTrade: Supabase stores the database, Xendit handles GCash payments, and an email service sends codes and alerts.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use ad trackers.

4. Your browser

When you sign in, a sign-in token is saved in your browser so you stay signed in. It is removed when you sign out. The feedback form is run by Google Forms, and Google's own privacy policy applies there.

5. How long we keep it

ID pictures and selfies are deleted right after the admin's decision. Trade, payment, and dispute records are kept so disputes can be checked and the law followed. The exact time will be set before SecureTrade handles real money.

6. How we protect it

Passwords are stored hashed. Creating an account, resetting your password, and changing your email need a code sent to your email. ID pictures can only be opened by admins. When SecureTrade is online, all connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you can ask to see the data we have about you, correct it, or have it deleted when we no longer need to keep it. You can also object to how it is used, and complain to the National Privacy Commission. To ask, use the Support page after signing in.

See also the Terms of Service.
